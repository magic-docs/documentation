# Privacy Policy for Docs Indexer

Last updated: June 1, 2026

## Overview
Docs Indexer is a desktop application that helps users process local markdown and text files into structured data.

## Information We Process
The app may process:
- Documents you choose from local folders (for example, `.md` and `.txt` files)
- App settings (such as selected provider, model name, source folder, output folder)
- API credentials required to call third-party AI providers

## How Data Is Used
Data is used only to:
- Run document mining and indexing features
- Generate output files you request (for example, a local SQLite database)
- Save local app preferences

## Data Storage
- App settings and outputs are stored locally on your device.
- API credentials are stored locally using the app's configured storage method.
- We do not run our own cloud backend for this app.

## Third-Party Services
If you configure a third-party AI provider (for example, OpenAI, Anthropic, or Google), selected document content may be sent to that provider to fulfill app features. Their privacy terms apply to data sent to their services.

## Data Sharing
We do not sell your personal data. We do not share your data except when required to provide app functionality through the third-party providers you configure.

## Security
We use reasonable measures in the app design to protect locally stored settings and credentials, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

## Children's Privacy
This app is not directed to children under 13.

## Your Choices
You can:
- Control which folders and files are processed
- Remove output files and local settings from your device
- Stop using third-party providers by removing API credentials from the app settings

## Changes to This Policy
We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date will reflect the current version.
